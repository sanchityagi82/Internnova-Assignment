SUPERSTORE SALES & PROFITABILITY ANALYSIS – FINAL PROJECT REPORT

1. Project Overview
This project applies an end-to-end analytics workflow to the Sample Superstore dataset.

2. Dataset
The dataset contains 9,994 records and 21 columns, including order information, customer details, geography, product categories, sales, quantity, discount and profit.

3. Data Preparation
The dataset was inspected for data types, missing values and duplicate records. Dates were converted to datetime format and duplicate rows were removed where required. No missing values were found in the supplied dataset after inspection.

4. EDA
Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
Total Profit: $286,397.02
Profit Margin: 12.47%
Unique Orders: 5,009
Unique Customers: 793

5. Key Findings
Technology is the largest sales category and also contributes the highest total profit.
The West region has the highest sales.
Tables are the least profitable sub-category in the dataset.
The correlation between Discount and Profit is negative, indicating that higher discounting is associated with lower profitability in this dataset; correlation does not by itself establish causation.
Sales and Profit have a positive relationship.

6. Power BI Dashboard
The dashboard should contain KPI cards, category/region charts, a monthly trend, top-product analysis and slicers for Year, Region, Category, Sub-Category and Segment.

7. Recommendations
Review discounting and margins for low-profit sub-categories. Examine successful category and regional strategies and assess whether they can be applied to weaker areas.

8. Conclusion
The project demonstrates data cleaning, EDA, visualization, dashboarding and business interpretation using a real-world retail dataset.
